The Pain Free way

Our 5-step pain free technique

Treatment here is designed around your comfort. Here is exactly how we make local anaesthetic as gentle as it can be.

1

We use a special gel which numbs the gum prior to receiving treatment.

2

We warm the local anaesthetic, which ensures it is a lot more comfortable.

3

We use only the shortest and finest needles so you don't feel them.

4

We inject very slowly, which makes the whole process gentler for you.

5

We use a computer based delivery system for the local anaesthetic.

Our promises to you

When you sit in our chair, here is what you can count on at every appointment.

  • We will keep you comfortable using our pain free technique at every visit.
  • We will listen first and explain your options clearly before any treatment.
  • We will respect your time and run a calm, well-organised practice.
  • We will treat you without judgement, whatever the state of your teeth.
  • We will care for your whole family, from first teeth to later life.

Non-Judgemental Dentistry

All of your dental treatments are performed in a non-judgemental environment, so you are completely relaxed and stress free. If it has been a while since your last visit, that is absolutely fine. We are here to help, not to lecture.
