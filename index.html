The Pain Free approach

Five things we do so your treatment stays comfortable

The small, deliberate steps that make a real difference to how an appointment feels.

1

We use a special gel that numbs the gum before any treatment begins.

2

We warm the local anaesthetic, which makes it far more comfortable.

3

We use only the shortest, finest needles so you barely notice them.

4

We inject very slowly, which keeps the whole process gentler for you.

5

We use a computer-controlled delivery system for the local anaesthetic.
