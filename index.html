What we do

Everything one practice should offer, under one roof

From a child's first check-up to a full smile makeover, you see the same familiar faces each visit. Here is what we look after.

Invisalign & clear aligners

Discreet, removable aligners planned and overseen by an Invisalign Key Opinion Leader. Suitable for teens and adults.

Dental implants

Replacement for single, multiple or full-arch missing teeth, planned and placed in-house at our St Andrews implant clinic.

Composite bonding

Tooth-coloured composite shaped to repair chips, close small gaps and refine the appearance of front teeth.

Veneers & smile makeover

A considered, step-by-step plan to address the look of your smile, discussed fully with you before any treatment begins.

General & family dentistry

Routine examinations, hygiene visits, fillings, crowns and dentures for every member of the family.

Cosmetic & whitening

Professional whitening and cosmetic treatments delivered gently, with comfort techniques at every stage.

A credential worth knowing about

Treated by an Invisalign Key Opinion Leader

Invisalign recognises a small group of dentists as Key Opinion Leaders, clinicians whose clear-aligner experience is drawn on to guide and teach others in the field. Stuart Farmer holds that recognition, and he treats patients here in St Andrews every week.

  • Clear-aligner planning overseen by a recognised Invisalign KOL
  • Implant treatment planned and placed in-house, not referred out
  • Gentle, step-by-step care with comfort techniques throughout
  • One consistent clinical team that follows your case from start to finish

Why independent matters

Owner-operated since day one, never sold to a chain

Many practices now belong to large corporate groups. We have stayed independent and single-site for forty years, and that shapes the experience you have with us.

St Andrews Dental Care

  • The founding dentist still treats patients here
  • One practice, one team, decisions made locally
  • You see the same familiar faces over the years

A typical corporate group
